title: Journaling: Five Benefits, How to Start, and What One Month Can Change
---

### 1. Avoid making the same mistake again

**The KPT method**

**P: Write down the problem**

- I lost time scrolling on my phone today
- I couldn't contribute to the team in the meeting
- I lost a sale
- I let my emotions get the better of me

**T: Write down something to try**

- As soon as I get home tomorrow, put my phone in the mailbox, out of reach
- Introduce journaling to help handle my emotions
- Prepare thoroughly for the sales meeting and go well beyond expectations: “You did all this for us?”

### 2. Reduce procrastination

1. Record small things that gave you a sense of accomplishment, building it up over time
2. Set priorities

### 3. Change your self-image

Write down the person you would like to be: physically, professionally, and in character\
Let these internal motivations lead to actions

For example: not using my phone at home → having self-control

### 4. Put experiences into words

- **What happened**: describe the situation objectively, from a third-person perspective, without judgment
- **Feelings**: how the experience changed your emotions
- **Dig into why**: keep asking why to find the underlying cause
- **Core insight**: what you realized and what it taught you
- **Next action**: take action using KPT

### 5. Find what you want to do

Write freely\
Record things you enjoy in everyday life\
And the people you respect\
Gradually shape your values

### Three mistakes to avoid

1. Never looking back at previous entries: notes that don't get used
2. Writing to look good instead of being honest about your actual state of mind
3. Feeling satisfied just because you wrote: journaling is a way to move from where you are toward who you want to be, not the goal itself

### How to keep journaling

1. Start small—even one minute counts
2. Use it well: experiencing its feedback and changes naturally gives you a reason to return
3. Share with someone you trust
