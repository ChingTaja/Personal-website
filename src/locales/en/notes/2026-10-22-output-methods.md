---
title: Three Powerful Output Methods to Make Knowledge Stick
---

Practice explaining one section of a book

**① Compress it in your own words**

Condensed notes: organize what you have learned by asking, “What does this mean in a nutshell?”

Point: delete / rephrase / leave things out

**② Keep a record of your feelings**

Take a sentence you read\
Write down your reaction: interesting, dislike it, and so on

**③ Reproduce it on a blank sheet in one minute**

Close the book\
Set a one-minute timer and write down the ideas you can remember

**④ Check your answers with a red pen**

Look for missing points or mistakes\
Review them or explore them further
