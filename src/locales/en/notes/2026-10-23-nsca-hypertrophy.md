Notes on the video's discussion of the updated NSCA *Essentials of Strength Training and Conditioning* and changes to the previous edition's hypertrophy recommendations

### 1. Rest between sets

- **Multi-joint exercises**: 2–3 minutes recommended
- **Single-joint exercises**: 60–90 seconds recommended

An exception mentioned in the video: women generally recover better between sets than men\
If 1–2 minutes is enough to recover, a shorter rest may work

### 2. Reps per set

- **Beginner**: 8–12 reps with lighter weights and more repetitions, to help avoid injury
- **Intermediate**: 6–12 reps, adding weight after becoming familiar with the movement
- **Advanced trainee**: 3–12 reps

### 3. Training intensity

**At or close to failure**

The video describes RPE (Rating of Perceived Exertion) scores of 6–10 as effective for hypertrophy

On a scale from 1 → 10, higher scores mean closer to failure

| RPE | State |
| --- | --- |
| 10 | Failure |
| 9 | Close to failure; 1 rep left |
| 8 | Close to failure; 2 reps left |

### 4. Myth: free weights versus machines

The video says there is no significant difference between the two for hypertrophy

Free weights, however, can help practice finer movement control and strengthen core stability
