## Summary

- **Purpose**: notes aren't just a warehouse of information. They are a digital laboratory for connecting ideas across life and improving independent thinking and action.
- **The four P.A.R.A. categories**:
  1. **Projects**: short-term efforts with a clear deadline in work or life.
  2. **Areas**: ongoing responsibilities without a fixed endpoint, such as finances, health, relationships, or client notes.
  3. **Resources**: fragments of knowledge, industry developments, or inspiring topics that aren't needed right now.
  4. **Archives**: completed or inactive material from the other categories, kept in reserve.
- **Processing and distilling information**:
  - Separate capturing information from organizing ideas.
  - Use four layers of progressive summarization: original text → bold key sentences → highlight keywords → extract a summary, making the note easy to scan.
- **Moving information when needed**: when a real need arises, move material from Resources, Areas, or Archives into a Project, turning stored knowledge into action.
- **Habits and compounding**:
  - **Project checklists**: spend 20–30 minutes at the start clarifying materials and goals, and 10–15 minutes at the end organizing intermediate work and archiving it.
  - **Regular reviews**: clear and sort the inbox weekly; update projects, review areas, and revisit a wish list monthly.
  - **Share knowledge**: turn insights into writing, videos, or conversations, creating feedback and benefits that build over time.

## My Thoughts

### Will I use what I save?

What matters to me is whether I can find and use something again after saving it

I don't have to decide its category once and for all. Gathering related notes when there's something real to do feels more flexible

### Capture first, organize later

I want to separate recording from organizing, without deciding where every sentence belongs the moment I save it

When I revisit it, I can choose the few lines worth keeping. That might also make it clearer why I saved it in the first place

### It doesn't have to be perfect immediately

I want these notes to help me find things and write

If maintaining categories takes too much time, I'd want to make the system simpler
