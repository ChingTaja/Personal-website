## Summary

- **What sensitivity means**:
  - Sensitivity is **raising the resolution of how we see the world**.
  - Through suitable clothing, care for others, and noticing subtle feelings, being “free from confusion” doesn't mean never feeling uncertain; it means knowing and accepting the uncertainty.
- **Seven everyday practices**:
  1. **Observe**: become aware of handling several things together, such as a small side task during a main one, and describe the taste of tea, wine, or coffee in words.
  2. **Organize**: simplify and keep things that carry personal meaning.
  3. **Change perspective**: consider another person's, a third party's, or the opposite position to reveal blind spots.
  4. **Stay curious**: follow what excites you without requiring it to serve work, reconnecting with people and places.
  5. **Decide**: develop personal standards and say why you chose something.
  6. **Reflect in writing**: write down advice, even from someone you dislike, to step back from emotion and consider it calmly.
  7. **Build values**: let many small decisions gradually shape your core values.

## My Thoughts

### Notice a little more in everyday life

I like describing sensitivity as resolution

With the same cup of tea, trying to describe the taste might reveal more than just saying I like or dislike it. I want to start with little things like that

### Liking something is enough reason to explore it

The part about usefulness to work narrowing our view reminds me that an interest doesn't always need a purpose first

Sometimes simply wanting to learn more is already a good enough reason

### Don't decide I can't before trying

If I decide something doesn't suit me before trying it, I might never discover whether I like it

I want to leave room to try and ask whether a choice comes from genuinely not wanting something, or from being afraid I won't do it well

## Next Steps

- [ ] **Observe everyday life**: look at overlooked corners and pay attention to bodily movements such as standing up and walking.
- [ ] **Practice taste and language**: describe a cup of tea, wine, or coffee in detail through words or a diagram.
- [ ] **Ask why ten times**: choose a question or sticking point and keep asking why until I reach something fundamental.
- [ ] **Say why I chose**: put the reasons behind today's decisions into words.
- [ ] **Reflect before bed**: take time alone to ask what I learned and felt, then plan three things to keep and three to adjust next month.
