## Summary

- **Where mental fatigue comes from**:
  - Rest isn't real rest if the mind can't rest. The book connects fatigue and stress with regret about the past and anxiety about the future.
  - Excessively task-driven “must” thinking is described as a cognitive distortion that creates mental fatigue.
- **Mindfulness and changes in the brain**:
  - The book describes mindfulness meditation as easing fatigue and reducing cortisol, and discusses changes in brain structure and function over eight-week programs such as MBSR.
  - It presents mindfulness in relation to cognitive behavioral approaches and awareness of thinking habits.
- **Responding to thoughts and feelings**:
  - **Observe from outside**: thoughts are trains; I am the platform. Thoughts are not the self.
  - **Label thoughts**: set them aside or acknowledge them without judging them, and consider counterexamples.
  - **Accept things as they are**: bring anxiety to mind and quietly repeat “this is how the world is,” practicing acceptance.
- **Ways to rest**:
  - Rest needs planning and methods, like the gaps between logs that keep a fire burning.
  - Everyday basics: sunlight, nature, and stretching.
  - A simple five-day approach: don't cling to the plan, visit somewhere new, and find a way of resting that suits you.

## My Thoughts

### Does rest need to produce something too?

I want to remind myself that resting doesn't have to mean accomplishing something

If I judge even doing nothing by its efficiency, I may just be putting pressure on myself in a different way

### I don't have to follow every thought

The image I remember is “thoughts are trains, and I am the platform”

Next time I keep going over the same thing, I want to notice that the thought has returned, without immediately following it all the way

### When things don't go as expected

When something makes me angry, I want to notice whether the problem is the situation itself or my wish for it to follow my plan

I might not know immediately, but I can at least pause before deciding how to respond
